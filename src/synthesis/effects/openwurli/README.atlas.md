# OpenWurli source

The OpenWurli oscillator type and the OpenWurli Amp effect run a C++ port of
OpenWurli 0.9.0 by hal0zer0, a physical model of the Wurlitzer 200A electric
piano, published under the GPL v3 or later at
https://github.com/hal0zer0/openwurli (commit 531680b, `crates/openwurli-dsp`).
OpenWurli builds some stages with Melange, also GPL v3 or later
(https://github.com/hal0zer0/melange at 7ecb36c, the revision OpenWurli pins):
its biquad comes from `melange-primitives`, and the tremolo oscillator is a
circuit solver generated by `melange-solver`, which carries Melange's own
code. OpenWurli is not affiliated with Wurlitzer.

The original is Rust. Every file here is a translation, in double precision,
with each expression evaluated in the original's order, so on macOS the port
renders bit-identical samples to OpenWurli built by rustc (see Testing). The
generated data, the pickup's field-solved capacitance tables, the MLP weights
and the tremolo circuit's matrices, were copied as literal text.

## How Atlas splits the instrument

In a 200A every key's reed swings in front of one shared pickup comb, and all
of them feed one amplifier. OpenWurli's engine (`engine.rs`) sums its voices
and runs that sum through one chain. Atlas splits the two halves the same way:

- Per voice, the oscillator type (`src/synthesis/producers/openwurli_source.*`):
  `wurli_voice`, the modal reed (`reed.rs`), the hammer's dwell filter, onset
  and attack noise (`hammer.rs`) and the electrostatic pickup (`pickup.rs`,
  `pickup_tables.rs`), with the per-key tables (`tables.rs`, `variation.rs`)
  and the optional MLP corrections (`mlp_correction.rs`, `mlp_weights.rs`).
- Shared, the effect (`src/synthesis/modules/openwurli_amp_module.*`):
  `wurli_amp`, the 2x oversampled preamp (`dk_preamp_legacy.rs`,
  `oversampler.rs`) with the tremolo's LDR inside its feedback loop
  (`tremolo.rs`, `gen_tremolo.rs`), the volume network (`tables.rs`), the power
  amp (`power_amp.rs`, its default behavioural model) and the speaker
  (`speaker.rs`).

The oscillator's output is the pickup voltage times 100. The amp divides its
input by 50, so an oscillator at its default level (70.7%, 0.5 after the level
curve), centred, drives the preamp with the pickup's real voltage, and the amp
adds OpenWurli's fixed +14 dB output alignment. The amp is mono, as the
instrument is: it plays the middle of its input and sends the result to both
channels.

## What changed

- Atlas plays every MIDI note. The reed of the nearest of the 200A's 64 keys
  (A1 to C7) gives the timbre and the voice gives the pitch: `Voice::setPitchRatio`
  (an Atlas addition) scales every mode's frequency from how the note was
  struck, so pitch bend, glide, transpose and tuning move the reed. A ratio of
  exactly 1 leaves the output bit-identical to OpenWurli.
- The oscillator's voicing controls are OpenWurli's own calibration inputs,
  which its plugin does not expose (`tables::CalibrationConfig`): Bark is
  `swing_scale` (2^(2 x Bark - 1), half to double), Air Damping is
  `air_drag_scale`, Treble Voicing shifts `voicing_slope` by its decibels over
  the 36 semitones from C4 to C7, and Register Balance off is `zero_trim`. Two
  are Atlas additions: Condition widens the per-reed tuning offsets of
  `variation.rs` from +-3 to +-14 cents (its "Condition" TODO), and Pickup Low
  Cut makes `pickup.rs`'s compile-time C-2 fork a switch (880 or 1436 Hz).
  Atlas also holds a note's swing within its pickup table's reach (4.29 mm for
  reeds 1-20, 2.89 mm above, as `tables.rs` states), where the Rust would run
  off the table; no default reaches it. At their defaults every control leaves
  the output bit-identical to OpenWurli's.
- Each Atlas voice holds one reed. A note struck on a sounding voice, or a
  killed voice, fades the old reed out over 5 ms, as `engine.rs` crossfades a
  stolen voice. Hammer noise is seeded from the key and a running strike count,
  as in `engine.rs`.
- In Reed envelope mode (the default) the reed's own decay and damper shape the
  note and hold the voice open until it is 80 dB down (`Voice::is_silent`);
  Envelope 1 mode hands that to Env 1.
- Atlas's sustain pedal and voice allocation replace `engine.rs`'s. The R-11
  "reed bar" trimmer stays at its factory default, as in the OpenWurli plugin.
- `AmpChain::reset` restores the state saved after the warm-up instead of
  running OpenWurli's 0.6 s warm-up again, so a reset on the audio thread is a
  copy. `AmpChain::resetAndWarmUp` is OpenWurli's reset.
- The preamp's thermal-noise switch is not ported: OpenWurli's default preamp
  ignores it.
- Not ported: the alternative solvers behind features OpenWurli does not ship
  by default (`gen_preamp.rs`, `gen_power_amp.rs`, the legacy sine tremolo),
  the calibration bench options, and test-only code.
- Per-key note parameters, the pickup level proxy's DFT and the Simpson
  integrals are cached in tables built once (`prepareVoiceTables`, called when
  an OpenWurli oscillator is created), with the same expressions, so a note-on
  costs about 1 µs.
- macOS's libm rounds `pow(2, x)` and `exp2(x)`, `pow(10, x)` and `__exp10(x)`,
  and separate `sin`/`cos` and `__sincos_stret` differently for a few arguments,
  and rustc's LLVM picks the second of each pair. The port calls those
  functions explicitly (`wurli_math.h`); elsewhere it
  falls back to the standard functions and is close to, not bit-identical with,
  the original.

## Build

These files are listed in `ATLAS_OPENWURLI_SOURCES` in `CMakeLists.txt` and
built outside the fast-math unity build with `-ffp-contract=off`: an FMA
contraction alone moves samples by about 1e-17, which the reference tests
catch.

## Testing

`AtlasOpenWurliTests` (`tests/openwurli_tests.cpp`) covers the oscillator and
the effect through the plugin. With the opt-in `third_party/openwurli` and
`third_party/melange` submodules, `tests/openwurli/build_reference.py` builds
the untouched Rust with `rustc` into a reference renderer
(`tests/openwurli/reference.rs`), and four more tests run:

- `--voice-reference`: single reeds across the keyboard, velocities, sample
  rates, MLP on and off, note-offs, noise seeds and voicing settings (the
  CalibrationConfig fields, and the C-2 fork through a second copy of the
  crate built with it flipped), rendered in several block sizes, must equal
  OpenWurli's `Voice` sample for sample
  (`tests/openwurli/voice_reference_tests.h`).
- `--amp-reference`: the chain on chords, sweeps, impulses and hot inputs, at
  several rates (with and without oversampling) and settings, must equal
  OpenWurli's shared chain sample for sample
  (`tests/openwurli/amp_reference_tests.h`).
- `--oscillator-reference` and `--engine-reference`: an Atlas patch, the
  oscillator alone or into the amp, all at their defaults, against OpenWurli's
  `Voice` or `WurliEngine` playing the same note. They agree to float precision
  (a residual below 1e-6) from the second block on: every Atlas voice ramps its
  amplitude up across its first block, and Atlas's master volume at 0 dB is a
  gain of 1.0013.

Measured on an M5 Pro: a reed costs about 0.7 ms per second of audio (0.07% of
one core) and a note-on about 1 µs; the amp about 3% of one core at 44.1 to
96 kHz, 18 KB, and 45 ms to prepare (on the effect preparation worker).
