## What changed since beta 2

### New

- **Particles effect.** A granular texture processor, in the Space category, built on Mutable Instruments' granular processor (MIT). It records what comes in into a buffer and plays it back as overlapping grains. Its controls:
  - **Mode.** Granular sows grains from the buffer; Pitch shifter and time stretcher plays it back continuously, splicing where the waveform matches; Looping delay replays it as a delay that loops and pitch shifts; Spectral freezes and reshapes its spectrum.
  - **Quality** sets how much sound the buffer holds: about 1 second in 16-bit stereo, 2 in 16-bit mono, 4 in 8-bit stereo and 7 in 8-bit mono, the 8-bit settings sounding grainier.
  - **Input Gain** (−18 to +6 dB), **Position**, **Size**, **Pitch** (±48 semitones), **Density** (negative sows grains at a steady rate, positive at random, 0 none), **Texture**, **Mix**, **Stereo Spread**, **Feedback**, **Reverb**, **Freeze** and **Trigger**. What Position, Size, Density and Texture do changes with the mode, and each one's description says how.
  - Every note played fires a grain, and so does Trigger, an Off/On slider, each time it turns on (modulation turns it on above 50%), so with Density at 0 grains play only when triggered.
  - Every slider, and Freeze, can be modulated from its context menu.
- **Saturator, Dynamic Tube and Overdrive effects**, in the Distortion and character category, and **Multiband Dynamics**, in Dynamics: native ports of Alessio Scala's JSFX, which recreate the Live devices of those names from measurements. The DSP is the scripts' own, checked sample for sample against them, and every control keeps its script's range and units.
  - **Saturator.** Eight curves (Analog Clip, Soft Sine, Bass Shaper, Medium Curve, Hard Curve, Sinoid Fold, Digital Clip and Waveshaper), Drive, Color filters before and after the curve, Post Clip, Output, Pre-DC Filter, Hi-Quality 2x oversampling and Dry/Wet. Bass Shaper Threshold shows with the Bass Shaper curve and the Waveshaper group with the Waveshaper.
  - **Dynamic Tube.** Tubes A, B and C, Drive, Bias, Envelope with Attack and Release, Tone, Output, Hi-Quality and Dry/Wet.
  - **Overdrive.** A band-pass filter (Frequency and Width) into a clipper, with Drive, Tone, Dynamics and Dry/Wet.
  - **Multiband Dynamics.** Low, Mid and High band groups, each with Active, Solo, Input Gain, Above and Below thresholds and ratios (read as 1 : x), Attack, Release and Output Gain, below the Low Band and High Band switches and the two splits; Detection (RMS or Peak), Soft Knee, Time, Output and Amount in a Global group. Twelve factory FX presets come with it, from the script's own: Standard Multiband Comp, OTT, Multiband Gate, Split Band De-esser and more. The script's sidechain is not included.
  - Hi-Quality adds 3 samples of latency to Saturator and 4 to Dynamic Tube, and Overdrive's look-ahead adds 8 at 2x oversampling; the host is told, and the dry signal is delayed to match. Every slider can be modulated from its context menu.
- **A Simple view for each LFO.** Every LFO opens with two tabs, Simple and Custom, read as radio buttons: Enter shows a tab's view and the arrows move to the other tab. The Simple view has **Shape** (Sine, Triangle, Saw Up, Saw Down, Square), **Morph**, **Sync**, the one rate row that Sync uses and **Trigger Mode**. Morph runs from −100% to 100% and can be modulated per voice: Sine bends towards a triangle or a square, Triangle moves its peak until it is a saw, the saws curve and Square sets its pulse width. The custom shape is kept while the Simple view plays, and saved presets open in the Custom view and sound as before. In the Custom view, Sync is now a menu and comes before Frequency.
- **Free Running LFOs and random modulators.** LFOs have a Free Running sync type, and random modulators a third Sync Type choice (Trigger, Sync, Free Running). A free-running source keeps one phase, or one random stream, for every voice: a new note joins it where it is instead of restarting it, and it keeps time between notes. With no note playing, a free-running source routed to an effect or another global control keeps moving, so an LFO on an effect no longer stops when the last note's release ends. The random modulator's old Off setting, which the editor called Free running, is now named Trigger.
- **Your own default patch.** Settings has a Default patch group. It names your default patch or says that none is chosen; Browse default patch picks a patch from your Atlas folder to be the default, and Reset default patch, shown only while you have one, goes back to Atlas's own. Each new Atlas in a host starts from your default patch, as Initialize patch does. Undo takes either change back.
- **New shortcuts.** The grave accent (or § on a Mac's ISO keyboard) goes to Presets, backslash to Macros, Shift A to Arpeggiators, V to Voice and performance, X to Signal routing and R to Random modulation. The keyboard help in Settings lists them.
- **Download progress and Cancel download.** While a community preset or bank downloads, a Download progress bar and a Cancel download button appear after the Autoload toggle, below the preset list. The bar's name carries the percentage that has arrived, said again every couple of seconds while the bar or the button has the focus, and Cancel download stops the download, removes what had arrived and puts the focus back on the preset list. Both are hidden, and out of the Tab order, while nothing downloads. While a patch or a bank uploads, the Share form's Cancel is named Cancel upload.

### Changed

- **Atlas's default patch.** Every oscillator plays Basic Shapes, a wavetable of sine, saturated sine, triangle, saw, square and two pulse waves, and every oscillator goes to the main effects. Presets saved before keep the Filter 1 and Filter 2 routing they were made with.
- **The Braids oscillator is called Macro.** Its type, controls and help say Macro. Presets and host automation load as before.
- **LFO shape presets.** The LFO's shape preset menu, G and H in its keyboard editor and the shapes Performer inserts are now the six common waves: Sine, Triangle, Saw up, Saw down, Square and Pulse, in place of 35 shapes, several of them the same wave under another name.
- **The Trance gate editor.** The step shapes are Whole, Medium, Short, Ramp down and Ramp up. Left and Right choose a step, G and H change its shape, M mutes and unmutes it, Backspace resets the pattern to a new Trance gate's, and the grid and pattern length keys stay. The level, curve and Apply keys are gone; each step reads its shape, and says muted when it is.
- **The community's collections in the preset browser.** In Community presets and Community banks the Bank list and column are Author, the uploader, and Category is Style for presets and Genre for banks. The Library list and the Bank and Library columns, which could only say Community, are left out there; All presets and Favourites keep them. The first row of each list is named after it: All authors, All styles, All genres.
- **Shorter download and upload announcements.** A transfer is said with its name and size, as in Downloading CoolPiano, 1.1 GB, or Uploading bank Keys. A finished one says Downloaded, Imported bank or Uploaded and its name, and Cancel says Download cancelled or Upload cancelled.
- **The Share patch form's first style is None,** which uploads the patch with no style.

### Fixed

- **G goes to the Global group.** With Glitch Delay or Multiband Dynamics loaded, G went to the effect's own Global group instead of Atlas's.
- **Community presets with multi-samples are downloaded once.** Return on a downloaded preset that carries its instruments downloaded it again every time. Atlas now keeps the library's checksum of each download in `Community/Downloads.json` and downloads again only when the library has a new copy.
- **Community banks are imported once.** Return on an imported bank unpacked its presets, wavetables, samples, LFO and FX presets and impulse responses again. It now says the bank is already imported; a new copy on the library is downloaded and imported once, and a bank whose folder you deleted is imported again from the archive Atlas kept.
