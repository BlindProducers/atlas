## What changed since beta 1

### New

- **Speech oscillator.** A new oscillator type that sings the words you type, with the voice of eSpeak NG. Type the words into the slot's text box and press Enter: each note sings the next syllable, the notes of a chord sing one syllable together, and the words rewind when playback starts. Its controls:
  - **Mode.** Sung sings each note's pitch and holds the vowel while the key is down. Spoken keeps eSpeak NG's own intonation and plays the words straight through, with middle C at the voice's own pitch and other notes moving the whole contour.
  - **Split.** Each note takes the next syllable, the next word or the whole text.
  - **Language and Variant.** Menus over eSpeak NG's 150 languages and 104 voice variants, such as other speakers or the Klatt synthesizer.
  - **Formant** moves the voice's formants up or down in semitones without changing the pitch. **Breathiness** blends in a whisper of the same words, up to a full whisper. **Speed** sets how fast the consonants and unheld vowels play, 0% to 400%. **Vibrato Depth** and **Vibrato Rate** add a vibrato that fades in after each note.
  - **Amplitude Envelope.** Voice lets each syllable shape its note and keeps the voice open until its last consonant; Envelope 1 shapes the voice with Envelope 1, as the other oscillator types do.
  - Transpose, Tune, FM and ring modulation from other oscillators work as on the other types. FM bends the pitch at audio rate while the formants and the syllable's timing stay put.
  - While the host plays, the syllables follow the song position: starting playback or moving the playhead anywhere sings the syllable a play from the beginning would, loops included, and hosts that chase held notes are handled. A chord with notes added while a key is down keeps singing one syllable until every key is up. In mono Legato mode a note change keeps its syllable at the new pitch.
  - The words are saved in presets; where in the song the notes start is saved with the host's project.
  - The plug-in bundles now carry eSpeak NG's data (about 29 MB), so the installers and zips are larger than beta 1's.
- **Maim effect.** Maim by Arden Butterfield, in the Distortion and character category: the sound goes through a real codec and back, with Maim's circuit bends on the MP3 encoders. Its controls:
  - **Encoder.** Blade and LAME are the MP3 encoders, each with its own tone; Opus is the internet codec; GSM the 13 kilobit telephone codec; LPC a 2.4 kilobit vocoder, with its own **LPC Sample Rate** from 2 to 22.05 kHz.
  - **Bitrate** for MP3 and Opus, **Turbo**, **Tilt**, **Error** and **Mix** at the top of the slot.
  - For the MP3 encoders, Maim's bends in groups: Spectral (**Frequency Shift**, **Level Shift**, **Feedback**), Butterfly (**Straight**, **Crossed**), and MDCT (**Band Step**, **Band Invert** and **Window Step** for LAME; **Band Shift** and **Band Reverse** in place of Maim's band reassignment matrix).
  - For Opus, GSM and LPC, a Packet loss group: **Loss Period**, **Loss Width**, **Loss Jitter** and **Lost Packets** (Drop or Stick).
  - **Drive**, **Low Cut**, **High Cut** and **Makeup** in the Gain and filters group.
  - Changing the encoder or bitrate crossfades to the new one; the host is told the codec's latency, which follows the encoder. Each codec runs at its own rate behind a resampler, so the effect sounds the same whatever the oversampling.
- **Arpeggiator rates can be modulated.** Free Rate, Sync Rate and Time Mode are modulation destinations now. Every step the arpeggiator plays is a new note, so they take only sources that run on their own: LFOs, random LFOs, macros, the mod wheel and pitch bend. Assigning another source says why it is refused.
- **Multi-sample loads sample files of any length.** DecentSampler instruments that slice one long recording (Claustrophobic Piano's is over 134 million frames) loaded none of their samples. The fixed cap is gone; the memory budget still applies.

- **Community presets in the preset browser.** The Collection list has a Community row beside All presets and Favourites. It lists the patches shared on [atlas.blindproducers.org](https://atlas.blindproducers.org), the community preset library, with the uploader as the bank and the style as the category, so the Library, Bank, Category and Tags lists and the search narrow them as they do the presets on disk. Return on a row downloads the preset into the Community library (`Community/Presets/<uploader>/<style>` in the Atlas folder), checks it against the library's checksum and loads it; from then on it shows among all presets too, and the Downloaded column says which ones are here. The list is fetched in the background when the row is chosen and kept for ten minutes; Refresh preset list fetches it again. A preset not downloaded yet is never autoloaded while scrolling.
- **Community preset previews.** A shared preset whose uploader attached a recording says so in its details, with the recording's length. P on the row, or Play preview in its context menu, fetches the recording into `Community/Previews` in the Atlas folder the first time and plays it through Atlas's output beside whatever is playing; P again stops it, starting another row's preview stops the first, and Atlas says when a preview starts, stops and finishes.

### Fixed

- **Mod wheel and pitch bend on global parameters.** A route from a wheel to a parameter outside the voices, such as an effect's mix or the volume, moved only while a note played and stayed where the last voice left it. It now follows the wheel whether or not a note is playing.
- **VoiceOver stays on the control after prompts, dialogs and rebuilds.** Typing a value, adding modulation, loading a wavetable or an instrument, or showing the wavetable editor could leave VoiceOver at the top of the window. The screen reader now returns to the control it was on, or its replacement when the rows were rebuilt, and announcements made meanwhile wait for that move instead of being cut off. Escape closes the value prompt.
- **EQ, Mid Side EQ and Compressor rows are grouped by band.** Each EQ band has its own group (Low band, Band 1 to 4, High band; Mid Side EQ puts them in a Mid and a Side group). The compressor keeps Enabled, Enabled Bands, Attack, Release and Mix at the top of the slot, with Low, Mid and High band groups below, each saying when its band runs.
- **Windows build.** This is the first beta whose Windows build includes the Speech oscillator; linking eSpeak NG no longer puts its compatibility headers ahead of the system headers.

### Building from source

`git submodule update --init` now also fetches `third_party/espeak-ng`, which the build compiles together with its language data, and `third_party/opus` for the Maim effect. Maim's circuit-bent LAME and BladeEnc and libgsm are kept in the repository. eSpeak NG is GPL v3 or later, Opus BSD-3, LAME and BladeEnc LGPL, libgsm its own permissive licence; see `NOTICE` and `third_party/README.atlas.md`.
