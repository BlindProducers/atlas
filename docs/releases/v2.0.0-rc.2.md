## What changed since RC 1

### New

- **Destroy effect.** A new effect in the Distortion and character category: a native port of Alessio Scala's Serum 2 Overdrive JSFX, which recreates the Overdrive mode of Serum 2's Distortion with its filter section and its Stack feature. The DSP is the script's own, checked sample for sample against it, and every control keeps the script's range and units. Its controls: **Drive**, **Stack** (1 to 16 stages of high-pass, gain control and soft-knee clipper), **Filter** (Off, Pre or Post, a state-variable filter before or after the stages), **Filter Frequency** (read in Hz), **Filter Q**, **Filter LP-HP**, **Level** (read in dB) and **Mix**. The filter's three rows show only while Filter is Pre or Post. Destroy adds 7 samples of latency; the host is told, and the dry signal is delayed to match.
- **Frequency Shifter Mode 2.** The Frequency Shifter has a Mode menu. Mode 1 is the shifter as it was, so older patches load unchanged. Mode 2 is a native port of Alessio Scala's Serum 2 Bode JSFX, which recreates Serum 2's Bode frequency-shifter module with its feedback delay, checked sample for sample against the script. Its rows, labelled Mode 2, are **Shift**, **Range** (in Hz), **Direction**, **Width**, **Mono Input**, **Delay** (in milliseconds), **Feedback**, **Blur** and **Mix**, in the script's units. Mode 2 adds 11 samples of latency, told to the host and matched on the dry signal.
- **Sort presets.** A Sort presets list after the preset search chooses the order of the preset table: Name A to Z or Z to A, Newest or Oldest first, Author A to Z and Category A to Z (Style or Genre on the community's side, as the column is named there). Community presets and Community banks also offer Most downloaded; away from them the sort goes back to the name. The sort is remembered per instance like the filters, presets that tie are listed by name, and a column header pressed with the mouse still sorts, which the list then names even when it is not one of its choices. The keyboard help lists it.

### Changed

- **S and Shift S no longer sort the preset table.** Sort presets takes their place, and the help, the table's description and the keyboard help say so.
- **The Scream effect is gone; Scream stays as a filter model.** The effect had been hidden from every effect menu since Scream became a filter model in the filter section and in Filter FX. A saved slot that still names it passes audio through, and every other effect keeps its number, so presets load as before.

### Fixed

- **No DC from Saturator or Destroy.** Saturator's curves, and Destroy's last clipper, are symmetric, so lopsided material such as a driven pulse wave left an offset on the wet signal. Both now run a 5 Hz DC blocker on the wet signal before the mix; the distortion itself is unchanged.
