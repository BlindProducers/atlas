## What changed since RC 4

### Fixed

- **DC offset from Hard Sync.** On the Analog oscillator, a Sync amount that isn't a whole ratio ended every master cycle part way through the synced wave, which shifted the output off centre: about -16 dBFS on a held saw, and more on chords, since every note's offset adds up. Hard Sync now subtracts that offset and otherwise sounds the same.
- **DC offset from the filters.** The Analog, Ladder, Dirty, Digital, Formant, Phase, Scream and Ring Mod models saturate inside, and a saw could push their output off centre. A voice's filter now removes what these models leave below 5 Hz. Filter FX and Sample & Hold are unchanged.
- **DC offset from the effects.** Many effects shifted the output off centre: Analogue Rotary, Analogue Echo and Analogue Flanger at their default settings, Flanger, Tape and Filter FX when driven, and, depending on their settings, Chorus, Delay, Reverb, Phaser, Distortion, Erosion, Floaty Delay, the compressors, Limiter, Dimension Expander, Multiband Dynamics, the OpenWurli amp, Maim, Particles and the Frequency Shifter. The slot of each of these effects now removes what its effect adds below 5 Hz and leaves the dry signal alone.
